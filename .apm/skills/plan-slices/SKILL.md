# Plan slices

Read applicable guidance, agreed scope/ACs, relevant code/contracts and existing work. Resolve material uncertainty before treating a dependency or interface as settled.

Propose small usable outcomes, not file/layer checklists. Give each a stable ID, scope, observable exit checks, covered ACs or explicit existing-work references, dependencies and delivery order. Cover every AC without speculative work or redundant umbrella tickets. Record the plan in the task spec or a linked artifact; retain existing IDs, decisions and approvals.

Interdependent slices or unclear interfaces require independent **`plan-reviewer`** assessment before approval. In a parent workflow, return the plan and review need; its review can satisfy this requirement when it covers the exact plan and interactions. Do not dispatch a duplicate assessment. Standalone, obtain that conditional fresh assessment with applicable guidance and sources; resolve material corrections with the same reviewer while its basis remains valid. Missing reviewer/context leaves required review incomplete.

Present outcomes, order, coverage and remaining gaps, then stop for approval. A parent workflow owns its next stage. This helper grants no implementation, commits or external publication.
